# Plague Watch (early trial 0.3.1)

A co-op game for 1 to 4 players. You are the last town guards in a plagued medieval town. By day you search the villagers for hidden bites, lock up the bitten, feed the town and bar the doors. By night the dead come at the gates in waves and you hold the town until dawn.

There is no fixed end: hold out as long as you can. Every fifth night a beast comes with the dead. Bring it down, and the next morning the watch can ring the stand-down bell by the church to end the run as a win. More than half of the living guards have to ring it. Every guard alive at that moment is kept for their player's next run, with their stats and weapon skill.

Guards can be bitten too. A bitten guard has three ways to go: drink a plague herb, which may cure the bite; give up their body in the jail cell and come back as a fresh recruit; or hide the bite and do what the voice asks.

This is a rough early trial. Expect bugs.

## Download and run

1. Get the zip from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Unzip everything into one folder.
3. Run `PlagueWatch.exe`.

- Windows 64-bit only.
- Keep `PlagueWatch.pck` next to the exe. It holds the game.
- The build is unsigned, so Windows may warn about it.
- `PlagueWatch.console.exe` is the same game with a log window.
- The game starts in full screen. F11 switches to a window.

On the front menu, type your name and pick your weapon, then choose Play alone, Tutorial, Host or Join. The Tutorial is a solo run with a guide. It walks through one whole day and night in ten short steps, and nothing from it is written to your record.

## Playing together

1 to 4 players. Play alone, or one player hosts and the rest join by the host's address.

The host:

1. Chooses Host a game. Windows will ask to allow the game through the firewall, and the host must say yes.
2. Waits a moment while the game asks the router to open the port by itself. The lobby then shows the address to give to friends, and a second address for anyone in the same house or network.
3. Presses Start the watch when everyone is listed in the lobby.

Everyone else:

1. Types the host's address into the Host address box and presses Join.
2. Waits in the lobby for the host to start.

Good to know:

- Everyone must run the same build. The build number is on the front menu (this one is 0.3.1). A guest on another build is told so and turned away.
- The run does not begin until every guest has loaded.

## Your first day

1. **Search.** Walk up to a villager and hold E to search them for bites. Do this to everyone, every day.
2. **Arrest the bitten.** When you find a bite, tap E on that villager and choose Arrest. They walk to the jail. You can also carry anyone to the jail and hold E at its door to lock them in. Do it before night, or they will turn inside a house.
3. **Feed the town.** The town eats every day. Lift ration sacks from the south field with F, carry them to the granary and hold E at its door. A beacon stands over the place where the thing you carry belongs.
4. **Build.** Carry planks from the workshop to a gate and hold E to build a barricade.
5. **Sleep.** When you are tired, sleep in a barracks bed.
6. **Bar the doors.** Near dusk, hold E at each house door to bar it. A barred door keeps the dead out for a while.
7. **Hold the night.** The dead come at the gates in waves. Hold the town until dawn. A guard who goes down gets back up after a moment. If your guard dies, a villager is drafted at dawn and you play on as them.

If you are not sure what to do, read the NEXT line at the top of the checklist on the right of the screen.

There is one interact button. Tap it (E, or RB on a gamepad) on a villager, a door or another guard and a menu opens, as in a role-playing game: talk, search, accuse, give a job, arrest once you have found their bite, knock, reinforce the door, vote. Hold it for the main action: the search for bites, barring a door, storing food. In the menu, move to choose, E to pick, Shift to leave. On a gamepad that is the stick, RB and B.

## Controls

The game works with keyboard and mouse or with a gamepad. On-screen prompts name the buttons of whichever one you touched last. Gamepad buttons are named as on an Xbox pad.

### Keyboard and mouse

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Click | Light attack: a three-hit combo |
| Right click | Heavy attack: knocks the dead down. Hold it to charge |
| Q | Shield, or a shove kick with a weapon that has no shield |
| Space | Jump. Press it again in the air to go higher |
| Shift | Roll. Hold to sprint |
| Ctrl | Slide: trips the dead |
| E | Tap: a menu for the villager, door or guard in front of you. Hold: the main action |
| F | Lift a thing, a fallen zombie or a downed friend. Press again to put it down. Either attack throws it |
| X | Use the carried item, or rest when your hands are empty |
| V and B | Skill and second skill |
| G | Ping |
| Tab (hold) | Stats and move list |
| Esc | Pause and settings |
| F10 | Screen filter: off, flat, curved |
| F11 | Full screen or window |

### Weapons

You pick your weapon on the front menu and keep it for the whole run. The racks at the armoury show the five weapons, but they do not swap yours.

- Sword and shield: balanced. Three-hit combo, spinning charge, and a shield.
- Greatsword: slow, wide sweeps that throw the dead back. No shield.
- Twin daggers: very fast and short. Big damage on the sleeping and the fallen. Charge to rush through.
- Axe and shield: heavy single blows that break brutes. Charge to slam the dead off their feet.
- Crossbow: shoots where you look. Slow to reload. Charge for a bolt that passes through. It hits friends too.

### Skills

A guard carries one or two skills, on the two skill buttons. Every guard starts a run with a skill. Each skill has a post at the training yard in the south-west corner of the town.

- Take a skill from its post to practise it for this run. You can practise one skill at a time, and a practised skill comes back more slowly.
- Each time you use a skill for what it is for, that counts. After enough good uses it is mastered for good, and you can pack it on the first day of every later run.
- A guard who dies or turns loses the skills they carried.
- Some posts start locked. The dark ones want favour from the voice first, and the beast's wants a beast brought down.

Three examples: Rally cry mends and wakes every guard close by. Bulwark means nothing can hurt you for three seconds. Shepherd's whistle sends every villager near you running for home.

## Settings

The screen settings are in the pause menu (Esc, or Start on a gamepad). Your choices are kept for the next time you play.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| CRT screen | Off, flat or curved. Curved is the default. F10 also steps through them. |
| Full screen | On by default. F11 also switches between full screen and a window. |
| Picture | 16:9 by default, or 21:9 or 32:9. The wider ones only show more of the world to the sides. |
| HUD size | Fit (the game picks a size to suit the screen), 2x, 3x or 4x. Only the sizes your screen has room for are offered. |

## Troubleshooting

**Windows warns about the game.** The build is unsigned, so a warning from Windows is expected.

**The game does not start.** Unzip everything into one folder first, and keep `PlagueWatch.pck` next to the exe.

**A friend cannot join.** Check these in order:

1. Everyone runs the same build. The number is on the front menu. A guest on another build is told so.
2. The host said yes when Windows asked to allow the game through the firewall.
3. The host's lobby says what the router did. If it says the router would not open the port by itself, the host forwards UDP port 24680 to their PC.
4. If the lobby says the host's internet provider shares one address between homes, friends cannot reach that PC directly. Use a virtual network tool (Tailscale, ZeroTier, Radmin VPN) or let another player host.
5. The run has not started yet. A run that has begun cannot be joined, and a watch is full at 4 guards.

A join that gets no answer gives up after 12 seconds and says why.

**Players in the same house.** Use the "Same house or network" address in the host's lobby.

**The picture.** See Settings above: F10 changes the CRT filter, and Picture (for 21:9 and 32:9 screens) and HUD size are in the pause menu.

**Reporting a bug.** The log files are in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Plague Watch\logs`. `PlagueWatch.console.exe` is the same game with a log window.
